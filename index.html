Logo

Overview

Rationale

This logo is built as a personal monogram using the initials R and A. I wanted the mark to feel compact, structured, and adaptable instead of decorative. The angular form connects to my architecture background through sharp geometry, controlled spacing, and negative space. At the same time, the overall silhouette suggests a pointer or cursor, which connects the mark to interaction design and digital work. The final direction keeps the logo mostly black and white so it can work across a portfolio, resume, website, and small icon use without losing clarity.

Small Use

The square mark can work as a favicon or small icon because the shape stays recognizable without the full name.

Favicon / Small Icon

The mark remains clear at small sizes because of the bold square container and high contrast.

04

Color Palette

The palette supports the logo system without making the mark dependent on color. Black and white carry the main identity, while charcoal, off white, cream, and deep brown support backgrounds, navigation, and page accents.
